Typical per-deal terms: the investor funds 40% of total project cost (purchase plus renovation) and receives 50% of the profit. A lender funds the rest.

The power of cash: when an investor funds the entire project in cash, there is no lender to pay. That saves about $30,000 per project on average in lender costs. It also saves about 20 hours of loan paperwork per project, time that costs us money too. Every dollar saved goes straight to profit. An all-cash deal is simply more profitable.

Section 8 Rental Homes

We buy single-family homes for about $80,000–$160,000 and rent them through Section 8, the federal Housing Choice Voucher program. The local housing authority pays most of the rent directly to us, so cash flow is steadier than ordinary rentals.

  • Government-backed rent and strong tenant demand
  • Built on our renovation crews: a home can be held for rent instead of sold
  • A rental portfolio we can sell when the market rises

Why manufactured housing: the government needs this product, and we are ahead of the wave

  • Housing shortage: Freddie Mac estimated in November 2024 that the US is short about 3.7 million homes (as of Q3 2024).
  • Cost gap: per 2024 Census and HUD data, a new manufactured home costs about half as much per square foot to build as a new site-built home, excluding land.
  • New federal law: the 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act, signed July 11, 2026 (Public Law 119-101), removes the requirement for a permanent steel chassis, opening new options in design, transport, foundations and multi-story building. States must confirm within a year that they treat these homes equally for financing, titling, insurance, taxes and installation.
  • More financing: the same law raised FHA Title I loan limits for manufactured homes, and HUD published an action plan in October 2026 to put the building-code changes into effect.
  • Conventional mortgages: Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac run dedicated programs for high-standard manufactured homes (MH Advantage and CHOICEHome), aligned in February 2026. Under Duty to Serve, eligible loans on manufactured homes titled as real property can get pricing fees waived.
  • Florida: HB 399 (2026) requires that a manufactured home titled as real property be allowed by right in any zone that allows single-family homes. Hernando County, where we operate, must update its code by January 2027.
  • Other states: in January 2026 New York Governor Kathy Hochul announced dedicated state investment in manufactured housing, approved in the FY27 budget under MOVE-IN NY.

The homes are built in a factory, away from the weather, and installed quickly. We buy the land, handle permits, site work, wells and septic, and install new Champion homes through our own dealership. We then sell or rent them as affordable housing or to snowbirds.
